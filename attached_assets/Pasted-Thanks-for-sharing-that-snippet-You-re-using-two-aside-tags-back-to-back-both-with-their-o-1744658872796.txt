## 💡 Bonus Tip

Make sure the **main content** area beside the sidebar isn’t hidden behind it:

```jsx

  <Sidebar />
  <main className="flex-1 p-6 overflow-auto">
    {/* Page content here */}
  </main>
</div>
```

This keeps the layout **responsive**, readable, and avoids overlaps.
